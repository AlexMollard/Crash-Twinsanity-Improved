[Aku Aku invincibility (3 masks) also protects from explosions]
# Crash's damage handler (0x137510) ignores hits while the invincible bit is set - unless damage >= 50, and TNT/Nitro/bomb
# explosions deal 100 (damage flag 2), so an invincible Crash still died to them. The handler converts the damage byte to a
# float and back (a no-op); that is replaced by a move, and the 4 freed slots compute "small hit OR explosion" into t9,
# which the invincibility test then uses instead of "damage < 50". Other big hits (instant deaths) still go through.

00137594 00000000 8E590010   # nop             -> lw    t9,16(s2)    damage flags
00137598 46800020 33390002   # cvt.s.w         -> andi  t9,t9,2      explosion
0013759C 46000064 2E980032   # cvt.w.s         -> sltiu t8,s4,50     small hit
001375A0 44140800 0338C825   # mfc1 s4,f1      -> or    t9,t9,t8     invincibility blocks this hit
00137604 2E820032 0320102D   # sltiu v0,s4,50  -> move  v0,t9        (delay slot of the invincible check)

# grace-invincibility bits stay as they were: 1 time in 5 Crash vanishes for the whole scene and the gameplay after it.

[Game clock set to 60 Hz to match the 480p / 60 Hz output]
# The game sizes its per-frame work budget (0x192360: time left in the frame, used by the background loader) from a
# frame rate it picks at start-up from the PAL flag: 50. With the output switched to 60 Hz, a frame is 16.7 ms, not
# 20 ms, so wherever the loader stays busy (the hubs) it worked past the end of the frame and every fifth frame or so
# was shown twice - about 50 fps with judder. Rig: 57-61 of 300 hub frames doubled at 50, 1 of 360 at 60; level loads
# also got slightly faster (Classroom Chaos 9.0 s -> 8.0 s with Fast CDVD). The value has no other use.
0017D6F0 24050032 2405003C   # addiu a1,zero,50 -> addiu a1,zero,60   (frame rate passed to the game clock)
